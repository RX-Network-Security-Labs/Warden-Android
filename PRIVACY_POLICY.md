# Privacy Policy — Warden

**Last updated: August 1, 2026**

Warden is developed and maintained by RX Network Security Labs.

---

## Summary

Warden does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

---

## Data Collection

Warden collects **no data whatsoever**. This includes:

- No personal information
- No device identifiers
- No usage statistics or analytics
- No crash reports sent externally
- No location data transmitted
- No advertising data

## Internet Access

Warden has **no internet permission**. It is technically impossible for Warden to send any data anywhere. You can verify this by inspecting the source code or the APK manifest.

## Local Storage

Warden stores permission access logs **locally on your device only**, inside a private app database. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is never shared with anyone
- Can be deleted at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling Warden

## Third Party Services

Warden uses **no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking libraries**.

## Open Source

Warden is fully open source under the GPL v3 license. Anyone can inspect the source code to verify these claims.

Source code: [github.com/RX-Network-Security-Labs/Warden-Android](https://github.com/RX-Network-Security-Labs/Warden-Android)

## Children's Privacy

Warden does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
